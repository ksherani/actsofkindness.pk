Our Mission & Vision

Building a compassionate Pakistan where every community thrives

Our Mission

To mobilize Pakistan's youth as change-makers, driving sustainable impact through health awareness, volunteer service, and dignified support for the most vulnerable. We believe in the power of community to transform lives.

Our Vision

A Pakistan where kindness is the foundation of society—where youth-led initiatives create lasting change, communities support each other with dignity, and opportunity reaches everyone regardless of circumstance.

Our Programs

Making a real difference in communities across Pakistan

Ramadan Mehrbaan

Hundreds of thousands of meals are distributed during Ramadan among people which mainly included labours, taxi drivers, security guards and hospital staff.

R-Fusion Blood Donor Hotline

All cases are first authenticated by our officers and later the word is spread among donors, potential donor is instructed and given guidelines before they donate blood.

Spread Love With Bread

Acts of kindness after verification, provides ration packs to the deserving families each month. The number of ration packs depends upon the amount of budget available.

#WhoisEdhi

In memory of greatest philanthropist Sir Edhi, we provide wheel chairs, crutches and beds in government hospitals.

Walls Of Kindness

Multiple walls have been built and our team members frequently visit and place large quantities of clothes and shoes so that those in need can get them free of cost.

Help A Smile

We frequently organize events in old homes, orphanages, and street-served, social citizens and local communities.
